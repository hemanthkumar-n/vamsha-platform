# Vamsha Design Principle

A person is not a relationship.

A relationship depends on who is viewing.

Example:

Viewer = Hemanth

Divya Bharathi → Sister

Viewer = Yuvan

Divya Bharathi → Aunt

Viewer = Keerthi

Divya Bharathi → Sister-in-law

The same person may appear differently depending on viewer context.

This is the foundation of the Vamsha Relationship Intelligence (VRI) model.
