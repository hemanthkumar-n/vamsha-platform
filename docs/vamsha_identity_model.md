
# Vamsha Identity Model

## Person

Person is immutable.

A person is never duplicated.

---

Example

Person:
Sudha Rani

---

## Names

Primary Name

Aliases

Known As

---

Example

Primary:
Natakam Sudha Rani

Aliases:
Mamidi Sudha Rani

Known As:
Sudha

---

Mamidi

Doguparti

Buduri
